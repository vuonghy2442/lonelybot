1 · The component map — (b = distance to v, a = distance to u)

component state SAT state (4) witness state ┄ geodesic ridge a+b=13 ▬ reversible edges (7% opacity)

Every state sits at (b, a) with a+b ≥ 13 (triangle inequality). Both parities occur and edges can stay inside a layer: the move graph is not bipartite — it contains 3-cycles (raise c ; pull c ; slide c). The witness is the orange polyline along the green ridge — it is a geodesic: each of its 13 letters lowers b by exactly 1. The ridge contains exactly these 14 states — any 13-letter path must walk the ridge step by step (Δa=+1, Δb=−1 every letter), so the geodesic u→v is unique: every other u→v play has length ≥ 15 and leaves the ridge. Note what this panel cannot show: the distance profile of the witness is perfectly monotone, so the failure of the mountain shape is invisible here — it lives in the per-suit rung walk below.

2 · The witness's rung walk — why it is not a mountain

A mountain would descend in every suit until one turn, then ascend: all pulls before all raises. Instead the clubs climb 2→3→4→5 (raises m3–m5) and come back down 5→4→3→2 (pulls m7–m9) — a hill in the middle of the hearts/spades valley (hearts 4→3 … 3→4, spades 3→2 … 2→3). The raise of 5C (m5) is a raise before the later pull of 5C (m7) with a different base — the genuine same-card relocation — and the split's left leg (m0–m10) contains the three club raises, so no MountainSplit exists.
